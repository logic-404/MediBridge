CBHS International Health is part of the CBHS Group, originally founded as a health fund for Commonwealth Bank employees. Their OSHC product offers two distinct tiers with meaningful differences in outpatient benefit rates.

---

## Tiers Offered

CBHS offers **two OSHC tiers**:
1. **OSHC Standard** — Premium tier with 100% MBS for ALL outpatient services (unique in market)
2. **OSHC Essentials** — Base-level tier at 85% MBS for all outpatient services

**Critical difference**: CBHS Standard pays 100% MBS for specialist/pathology/radiology out-of-hospital — the only OSHC tier in the market that does this. All other insurers (including CBHS Essentials) pay 85% for non-GP outpatient services.

## Tier: OSHC Standard

### Out-of-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| GP services | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Specialist consultations | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Pathology | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Diagnostic imaging / radiology | **100% of MBS fee** |
| All other out-of-hospital medical services | **100% of MBS fee** |

**This is unique in the OSHC market.** CBHS Standard is the only tier that pays 100% MBS for ALL outpatient medical services, not just GP visits. For students who frequently see specialists, this eliminates the 15% MBS gap that exists with all other insurers/tiers.

### In-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| Medical services to admitted patient | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Public hospital (shared ward, same-day, A&E, ICU, outpatient, post-op) | 100% of rate set by State/Territory health authorities |
| Agreement private hospital | 100% of contracted charges (minimum shared ward) |
| Non-agreement private hospital | Minimum benefit per Benefit Requirement Rules |

### Prescription Medicines

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Co-payment | PBS patient contribution for general beneficiaries |
| Maximum per item | **$50** above PBS co-payment |
| Annual limit (Single) | **$300** per calendar year |
| Annual limit (Dual/Multi-Family) | **$600** per calendar year |

**Note**: CBHS Standard has **lower** annual pharmaceutical limits ($300/$600) compared to most other insurers ($500/$1,000). This is a trade-off for the higher outpatient benefit rates.

### Ambulance

100% of the charge for emergency ambulance transport when medically necessary.

### Medical Devices and Prostheses

100% of the cost as listed in the Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products Rules (Federal Government Prescribed List).

### Emergency Department Facility Fee

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| ED facility fee benefit | **$160** per visit |
| Applies to | Presentations to hospital emergency departments |

**Unique feature**: CBHS Standard provides a $160 benefit towards emergency department facility fees. No other OSHC provider offers this. This helps offset the ED presentation fee that some hospitals charge.

### Online Doctor

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Online doctor benefit | **100%** of consultation fee |
| Provider | CBHS telehealth partner |

CBHS Standard covers 100% of the cost of online doctor (telehealth) consultations through their partner service. This is included at no additional cost.

### Waiting Periods

| Condition | Waiting Period |
|-----------|---------------|
| GP services (specified MBS groups) | **Nil** |
| Emergency treatment | **Nil** |
| Ambulance | **Nil** |
| Psychiatric conditions (non-emergency) | **2 months** |
| Pre-existing conditions (non-psychiatric, hospital) | **12 months** |
| Pregnancy and birth | **12 months** (policy < 2 years) |
| All other services | **2 months** |

### Exclusions

**Deed exclusions (Schedule 3):**
1. Treatment outside Australia
2. Compensable injury/illness (workers comp, motor vehicle, etc.)
3. Treatment that is not medically necessary

**Insurer-specific exclusions:**
4. Assisted reproductive services, including IVF
5. Treatment arranged before arrival in Australia
6. Pre-existing condition treatment in first 12 months (unless emergency)
7. Dental, physiotherapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, naturopathy, optical (unless MBS-listed)
8. Non-PBS medications
9. Gap between MBS fee and doctor's actual charge
10. Services not on the MBS
11. Cosmetic surgery not medically necessary

## Cost Calculation Impact

### CBHS Standard

```
Out-of-hospital GP visit:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Out-of-hospital specialist/pathology/radiology:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%   ← UNIQUE: 100% not 85%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

In-hospital:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Pharmaceuticals:
  Student pays PBS co-payment
  OSHC reimburses up to $50/item above co-payment
  Annual cap: $300 (single) / $600 (family)  ← LOWER than most
```

### CBHS Essentials

```
Out-of-hospital GP visit:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 85%   ← LOWER than most (85% not 100%)
  Gap = Doctor_charge - (MBS_fee × 85%)

Out-of-hospital specialist/pathology/radiology:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 85%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - (MBS_fee × 85%)

In-hospital:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Pharmaceuticals:
  Student pays PBS co-payment
  OSHC reimburses up to $50/item above co-payment
  Annual cap: $500 (single) / $1,000 (family)
```

### Worked Example: Specialist Visit ($200 charge, MBS fee $101.30)

**CBHS Standard**:
- OSHC pays: $101.30 (100% MBS)
- Student gap: $200 - $101.30 = **$98.70**

**CBHS Essentials**:
- OSHC pays: $86.11 (85% MBS)
- Student gap: $200 - $86.11 = **$113.89**

Difference: **$15.19 more** out-of-pocket on Essentials for same specialist visit.

## Claims Process

1. **Online member portal**: Submit claims digitally
2. **Direct billing**: Available with selected providers
3. **Manual claim**: Submit receipts with membership number
4. **Reimbursement**: To nominated Australian bank account

---

## Transferring to CBHS

- Obtain clearance certificate from previous OSHC provider
- Waiting periods already served are credited (continuous cover required)
- No gap between previous and new policy permitted

## Key Definitions (CBHS-Specific)

- **Pre-existing condition**: Signs/symptoms existed in the 6-month period before OSHC start, determined by CBHS-appointed medical practitioner
- **Emergency Treatment**: 8 specific conditions as defined in the deed
- **Agreement hospital**: Hospitals in CBHS's contracted network
- **Online doctor**: Telehealth consultation with CBHS-partnered medical practitioner
