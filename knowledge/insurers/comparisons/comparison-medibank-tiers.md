# Medibank Private — Tier Comparison: Essentials vs Comprehensive

> This document compares the two OSHC tiers offered by Medibank Private.

---

## Overview

Medibank's two tiers share identical medical benefit rates. The differentiation is entirely in **pharmaceutical limits, mental health support, medical repatriation, and boarder fee**. Comprehensive is the premium tier for students who want maximum coverage for medications and additional safety-net features.

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

### Medical Benefits

| Feature | Essentials | Comprehensive | Difference |
|---------|-----------|---------------|------------|
| GP benefit (out-of-hospital) | 100% MBS | 100% MBS | None |
| Specialist benefit (out-of-hospital) | 85% MBS | 85% MBS | None |
| Pathology (out-of-hospital) | 85% MBS | 85% MBS | None |
| Radiology (out-of-hospital) | 85% MBS | 85% MBS | None |
| In-hospital medical | 100% MBS | 100% MBS | None |
| Public hospital | 100% charges | 100% charges | None |

No difference in medical benefit rates between tiers.

### Pharmaceutical Benefits — THE KEY DIFFERENCE

| Feature | Essentials | Comprehensive | Winner |
|---------|-----------|---------------|--------|
| Co-payment | $30 flat | $30 flat | Tie |
| Max per item | $70 | **Unlimited** (100%) | Comprehensive |
| Annual limit (Single) | $500 | **$1,000** | Comprehensive |
| Annual limit (Family) | $1,000 | **$2,000** | Comprehensive |

**This is the most significant tier difference.** Comprehensive has no per-item cap and double the annual limit. For expensive medications (e.g., specialty drugs costing $200+/script), this saves hundreds annually.

### Additional Features

| Feature | Essentials | Comprehensive | Details |
|---------|-----------|---------------|---------|
| Medical repatriation | **No** | **$100,000** | Transport home for serious illness/death |
| Mental health extras | **No** | **$200/year** | Non-MBS counselling/psychology |
| Boarder fee | **No** | **$150/admission** | Parent stay during child hospital admission |
| No excess | Yes | Yes | Deed mandated |
| Medibank app | Yes | Yes | Same features |
| Direct billing | Yes | Yes | Same network |

### Waiting Periods

| Condition | Essentials | Comprehensive |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| GP services | Nil | Nil |
| Emergency | Nil | Nil |
| Psychiatric (incl. pre-existing) | **Nil** | **Nil** |
| Pre-existing (non-psychiatric) | 12 months | 12 months |
| Pregnancy | 12 months | 12 months |
| Other | 2 months | 2 months |

No difference — both tiers waive psychiatric waiting periods (unique advantage over ahm, nib, CBHS).

### Exclusions

Same base exclusion list. Comprehensive additionally INCLUDES services that Essentials excludes:
- Medical repatriation (excluded in Essentials, covered to $100k in Comprehensive)
- Non-MBS mental health (excluded in Essentials, $200/yr in Comprehensive)
- Boarder fee (excluded in Essentials, $150/admission in Comprehensive)

## Cost Impact Analysis

### Scenario 1: Student on expensive specialty medication ($200/script, monthly)

Annual cost at pharmacy: $200 × 12 = $2,400

**Essentials**:
- Per script: $30 co-pay. Remaining $170. OSHC reimburses min($170, $70) = $70.
- Student pays: $30 + $100 = $130/script.
- Annual: $130 × 12 = $1,560. But capped at $500 annual benefit.
- After annual cap (~month 7): student pays full $200/script for remaining months.
- **Total annual student cost: ~$1,900**

**Comprehensive**:
- Per script: $30 co-pay. Remaining $170. OSHC reimburses 100% = $170.
- Student pays: $30/script.
- Annual: $30 × 12 = $360. Annual benefit used: $170 × 12 = $2,040. Cap is $1,000.
- After annual cap (~month 6): student pays full $200/script.
- **Total annual student cost: ~$1,350**

**Annual saving with Comprehensive: ~$550**

### Scenario 2: Student needing psychology sessions beyond MBS cap

10 non-MBS psychology sessions at $150 each = $1,500/year

**Essentials**: No coverage for non-MBS mental health. Student pays **$1,500**.
**Comprehensive**: $200 annual benefit. Student pays **$1,300**.
**Saving: $200** (the full annual benefit).

### Scenario 3: Healthy student, rare GP visits, no medications

4 GP visits/year, no medications.

**Essentials**: OSHC pays 100% of MBS. Student gap = $0 at MBS rate. Pharma benefit unused.
**Comprehensive**: Same coverage. Extra features unused.
**Saving: $0** — Comprehensive premium cost is wasted.

### Scenario 4: Medical emergency requiring repatriation

Student develops serious condition requiring repatriation to home country.

**Essentials**: No repatriation benefit. Student/family pays all transport costs (potentially $20,000–$100,000+).
**Comprehensive**: Up to $100,000 covered by OSHC.
**Potential saving: Up to $100,000** (rare but catastrophic risk).

---

## Decision Framework

| Choose Essentials if... | Choose Comprehensive if... |
|------------------------|---------------------------|
| Healthy, minimal medication needs | On regular or expensive medications |
| Budget-conscious | Want maximum pharmaceutical coverage |
| Low risk tolerance not a priority | Value repatriation safety net ($100k) |
| No dependant children | Have children (boarder fee benefit) |
| MBS-covered mental health sufficient | Need non-MBS mental health sessions |

## Comparison with ahm (Medibank Subsidiary)

Since ahm is a Medibank subsidiary, it's worth noting key differences:

| Feature | Medibank Essentials | Medibank Comprehensive | ahm OSHC |
|---------|-------------------|----------------------|----------|
| GP rate | 100% MBS | 100% MBS | 100% MBS |
| Specialist rate | 85% MBS | 85% MBS | 85% MBS |
| Pharma co-pay | $30 flat | $30 flat | PBS co-pay (~$31.60) |
| Pharma max/item | $70 | Unlimited | $50 |
| Pharma annual (single) | $500 | $1,000 | $500 |
| Psychiatric waiting | **Nil** | **Nil** | **2 months** |
| Repatriation | No | $100,000 | No |
| Mental health extras | No | $200/yr | No |

Medibank tiers have better pharmaceutical terms and waived psychiatric waiting compared to ahm.
