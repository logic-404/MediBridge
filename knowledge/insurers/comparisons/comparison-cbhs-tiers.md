# CBHS International Health — Tier Comparison: Standard vs Essentials

> This document compares the two OSHC tiers offered by CBHS International Health.

---

## Overview

CBHS is the only OSHC provider where choosing between tiers results in **fundamentally different outpatient benefit rates**. Most multi-tier insurers (Allianz, Medibank) keep medical rates the same and vary on extras. CBHS varies the core medical benefit itself.

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

### Medical Benefits

| Feature | Standard | Essentials | Winner |
|---------|----------|------------|--------|
| GP benefit (out-of-hospital) | **100% MBS** | 85% MBS | Standard |
| Specialist benefit (out-of-hospital) | **100% MBS** | 85% MBS | Standard |
| Pathology (out-of-hospital) | **100% MBS** | 85% MBS | Standard |
| Radiology (out-of-hospital) | **100% MBS** | 85% MBS | Standard |
| In-hospital medical | 100% MBS | 100% MBS | Tie |
| Public hospital | 100% charges | 100% charges | Tie |

**Key insight**: CBHS Standard is the ONLY OSHC product in the entire market that pays 100% MBS for ALL outpatient services. CBHS Essentials pays only 85% even for GP visits — worse than most other insurers which give 100% for GP.

### Pharmaceutical Benefits

| Feature | Standard | Essentials | Winner |
|---------|----------|------------|--------|
| Co-payment type | PBS co-pay | PBS co-pay | Tie |
| Max per item | $50 | $50 | Tie |
| Annual limit (Single) | $300 | **$500** | Essentials |
| Annual limit (Family) | $600 | **$1,000** | Essentials |

**Trade-off**: Standard has better medical but worse pharmaceutical annual limits. Students on expensive medications may prefer Essentials for the higher annual pharma cap.

## Cost Impact Analysis

### Scenario 1: Student who sees specialists frequently

Monthly: 2 specialist visits at $200 each (MBS fee $101.30)

**Standard**: OSHC pays $101.30 × 2 = $202.60. Gap = $197.40/month.
**Essentials**: OSHC pays $86.11 × 2 = $172.22. Gap = $227.78/month.
**Annual saving with Standard**: ~$364

### Scenario 2: Student on multiple medications

Monthly: 3 scripts at $80 each (PBS co-pay $31.60)

Per script: Each reimburses up to $50 above co-pay.
Cost per script: $31.60 co-pay. Remaining $48.40 — fully covered (under $50 cap).
Monthly pharma cost: $31.60 × 3 = $94.80.
**Annual pharma spend**: $1,137.60.

**Standard**: Annual cap $300. After cap exhausted (month 4), student pays full price. Total student cost = $300 benefit received, then $94.80/month × 8 remaining months = **$758.40 remaining**.
**Essentials**: Annual cap $500. After cap (month 6), student pays full. Total student cost = $500 benefit, then $94.80 × 6 = **$568.80 remaining**.
**Annual saving with Essentials**: ~$189 on pharma.

### Scenario 3: Healthy student, occasional GP visits

4 GP visits/year at MBS rate ($43.90 each)

**Standard**: OSHC pays 100% = $43.90. Gap = $0 (if doctor charges MBS).
**Essentials**: OSHC pays 85% = $37.32. Gap = $6.58 per visit × 4 = **$26.33/year**.

## Decision Framework

| Choose Standard if... | Choose Essentials if... |
|----------------------|------------------------|
| Frequent specialist visits | Low medication costs |
| Want zero gap at MBS-rate GPs | Price-sensitive, minimal healthcare needs |
| Value ED facility fee benefit | Need higher pharma annual limit |
| Want included online doctor | Don't mind 85% GP rate |
| Outpatient costs > pharma costs | Pharma costs > outpatient savings |

---

## Market Context

- CBHS Standard's 100% outpatient rate is unique — no other insurer/tier offers this
- CBHS Essentials' 85% GP rate is the lowest — most other insurers give 100% for GP
- CBHS tiers represent the most meaningful tier differentiation in the OSHC market
