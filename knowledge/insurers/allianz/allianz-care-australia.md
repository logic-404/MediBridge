## Tiers Offered

Allianz offers **two OSHC tiers**:
1. **OSHC Essentials** — Base-level cover meeting deed minimums with medical repatriation
2. **OSHC Standard** — Identical medical benefits to Essentials, includes medical repatriation

Both tiers provide the same medical benefit rates. The difference is primarily in the product naming and positioning.

## Tier: OSHC Essentials

### Out-of-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| GP services (MBS Groups A1, A2, A7 sub 2/10, A22, A23, A40 sub 1/2, A46) | **100% of MBS fee** |
| All other out-of-hospital medical services (specialists, pathology, radiology) | **85% of MBS fee** |

### In-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| Medical services to admitted patient (surgeon, anaesthetist, etc.) | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Public hospital (shared ward, same-day, A&E, ICU, outpatient, post-op) | 100% of rate set by State/Territory health authorities |
| Agreement private hospital | 100% of contracted charges (minimum shared ward) |
| Non-agreement private hospital | Minimum benefit per Benefit Requirement Rules |

### Prescription Medicines

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Co-payment | PBS patient contribution for general beneficiaries |
| Maximum per item | **$50** above PBS co-payment |
| Annual limit (Single) | **$500** per calendar year |
| Annual limit (Dual/Multi-Family) | **$1,000** per calendar year |
| Per-person sub-limit in family | Each individual has single-person limit until family max reached |

Only PBS-listed medicines are covered. Non-PBS medications, non-prescribed drugs, over-the-counter medicines are excluded.

### Ambulance

100% of the charge for emergency ambulance transport provided by an approved ambulance service when medically necessary for hospital admission or emergency treatment.

### Medical Devices and Prostheses

100% of the cost as listed in the Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products Rules (Federal Government Prescribed List).

### Medical Repatriation

**$100,000 maximum per policy**. Covers costs approved by an Allianz-appointed medical practitioner for repatriation of the student and dependants to their home country in the event of serious illness, injury, medical condition, or death.

### Waiting Periods

| Condition | Waiting Period |
|-----------|---------------|
| GP services (specified MBS groups) | **Nil** |
| Psychiatric pre-existing conditions | **Nil** (no waiting for care or treatment for a psychiatric condition) |
| Emergency treatment | **Nil** |
| Pre-existing conditions (non-psychiatric, hospital) | **12 months** |
| All other services | No explicit additional waiting stated |

Note: Allianz explicitly states "No waiting period will apply if you receive: General Practitioner services; care or treatment for a psychiatric condition; or emergency treatment."

### Exclusions

**Deed exclusions (Schedule 3):**
1. Treatment outside Australia (except medical repatriation benefit)
2. Compensable injury/illness (workers comp, motor vehicle, etc.)
3. Treatment that is not medically necessary (including elective cosmetic surgery)

**Insurer-specific exclusions:**
4. Assisted reproductive services, including IVF
5. Transportation of student/dependants into or out of Australia (except medical repatriation benefit)
6. Treatment arranged before arrival in Australia
7. Pre-existing condition treatment in first 12 months (unless emergency)
8. Dental, physiotherapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, naturopathy, optical charges, and other ancillary services (unless they meet MBS requirements)
9. Non-PBS medications, drugs, or treatments not prescribed by a doctor
10. Co-payment (difference between MBS fee and doctor's actual charge)
11. Service fees not included in the benefits covered

**Summary**: Both tiers are functionally identical in terms of medical benefits, pharmaceutical limits, and exclusions. The distinction is primarily in product naming/positioning. Both include medical repatriation at $100,000.

## Cost Calculation Impact

For a student with Allianz (either tier):

```
Out-of-hospital GP visit:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee  (zero if doctor charges at MBS rate)

Out-of-hospital specialist/pathology/radiology:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 85%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - (MBS_fee × 85%)

In-hospital:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%  (for medical services)
  Hospital charges: covered at agreement hospital, minimum benefit at non-agreement

Pharmaceuticals:
  Student pays PBS co-payment first
  OSHC reimburses up to $50 per item above co-payment
  Annual cap: $500 (single) / $1,000 (family)
```

## Claims Process

1. **Via Allianz MyHealth app**: Upload photos of invoices/receipts
2. **Manual claim**: Download form from website, email to oshcclaims@allianzcare.com.au with policy number
3. **Post**: Send to Locked Bag 3001, Toowong QLD 4066
4. **Processing time**: 10 working days for completed claims
5. **Reimbursement**: Direct debit to Australian bank account, or telegraphic transfer to international bank if no longer in Australia

## Transferring to Allianz

- Student must obtain a clearance certificate from previous OSHC provider
- Waiting periods already served are credited (continuous cover required)
- No gap between previous and new policy permitted

## Key Definitions (Allianz-Specific)

- **GP Services**: Out-of-hospital services under MBS Groups A1, A2, A7 (sub 2, 10), A22, A23, A40 (sub 1, 2), A46
- **Emergency Treatment**: 8 specific conditions as defined in the deed (risk of serious morbidity/mortality, organ failure, drug overdose, psychiatric disturbance, severe pain, acute haemorrhage, etc.)
- **Pre-existing condition**: Signs/symptoms existed in the 6-month period before becoming insured, as determined by Allianz-appointed medical practitioner
- **Agreement hospital**: Hospitals specified at www.allianzcare.com.au/en/Find-a-Hospital.html
