## Tiers Offered

Bupa offers **one OSHC tier** for hospital and medical cover:
1. **OSHC** — Single tier covering all deed-mandated benefits

Additionally, Bupa offers **optional Extras add-ons** (general treatment) that can be purchased separately:
- Freedom 50
- Freedom 60
- Budget 60
- Your Choice 60
- Top 60
- Top 75
- Top 90

The Extras add-ons cover services like dental, optical, physiotherapy, chiropractic, and other allied health — these are NOT part of the base OSHC product and are purchased separately.

## Tier: OSHC (Hospital & Medical)

### Out-of-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| GP services | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Specialist consultations | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Pathology, radiology, diagnostic imaging | **100% of MBS fee** |
| All other out-of-hospital medical services | **100% of MBS fee** |

**Important note**: Bupa's brochure states that ALL outpatient medical services are "covered up to 100% of the Medicare Benefit Schedule (MBS) fee." This exceeds the deed minimum of 85% for out-of-hospital services. This applies to specialist consultations as well as GP visits.

### In-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| Medical services to admitted patient | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Public hospital (shared ward, same-day, A&E, ICU, outpatient, post-op) | 100% of rate set by State/Territory health authorities |
| Agreement private hospital | 100% of contracted charges (minimum shared ward) |
| Non-agreement private hospital | Minimum benefit per Benefit Requirement Rules |
| Private room in agreement hospital | Covered when medically necessary or only room available |

### Medical Gap Scheme

Bupa participates in the **Medical Gap Scheme (MGS)**. When a doctor participates in MGS:
- The doctor agrees to charge no more than a set amount above the MBS fee
- Bupa pays the gap between MBS benefit and the doctor's charge
- Result: **no out-of-pocket cost** for the student for medical services in hospital

When a doctor does NOT participate in MGS:
- Bupa pays 100% of MBS fee
- Student pays the gap between MBS fee and doctor's actual charge

### Prescription Medicines

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Co-payment | PBS patient contribution for general beneficiaries |
| Maximum per script | **$50** above PBS co-payment |
| Annual limit (Single) | **$500** per calendar year |
| Annual limit (Dual/Multi-Family) | **$1,000** per calendar year |

Only PBS-listed medicines prescribed by a registered doctor are covered. Over-the-counter, non-PBS, and non-prescribed medications are excluded.

### Ambulance

100% of the charge for emergency ambulance transport when medically necessary for hospital admission or emergency treatment.

### Medical Devices and Prostheses

100% of the cost as listed in the Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products Rules (Federal Government Prescribed List) — no gap for listed items.

### Waiting Periods

| Condition | Waiting Period |
|-----------|---------------|
| GP services (specified MBS groups) | **Nil** |
| Emergency treatment | **Nil** |
| Ambulance | **Nil** |
| Non pre-existing hospital admissions | **Nil** |
| Psychiatric conditions (including pre-existing) | **Waived** (until further notice) |
| Pre-existing conditions (non-psychiatric, hospital) | **12 months** |
| Pregnancy and birth | **12 months** (policy < 2 years) |
| Prescription medicines | **Nil** |
| All other services | **Nil** (non pre-existing) |

**Key differentiator**: Bupa has **waived the 2-month waiting period for psychiatric conditions** (including pre-existing psychiatric conditions) until further notice. This is unique among OSHC providers. The deed allows a 2-month wait for psychiatric hospital treatment — Bupa voluntarily removes it.

### Pre-Existing Conditions

A pre-existing condition is an ailment, illness, or condition where signs or symptoms existed in the **6-month period** before the student's OSHC policy began, as determined by a Bupa-appointed medical practitioner. The 12-month waiting period applies to hospital treatment for pre-existing conditions (except psychiatric, which is waived).

### Exclusions

**Deed exclusions (Schedule 3):**
1. Treatment outside Australia
2. Compensable injury/illness (workers comp, motor vehicle, etc.)
3. Treatment that is not medically necessary (including elective cosmetic surgery)

**Insurer-specific exclusions:**
4. Assisted reproductive services, including IVF
5. Services arranged before the student arrived in Australia
6. Pre-existing condition treatment in first 12 months (unless emergency or psychiatric)
7. Dental, physiotherapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, naturopathy, optical (unless MBS-listed or covered under optional Extras)
8. Non-PBS medications
9. Gap between MBS fee and doctor's actual charge (unless MGS applies)
10. Services not listed on the MBS (unless covered under Extras)
11. Cosmetic surgery that is not medically necessary

## Optional Extras Add-Ons

Bupa is the only OSHC provider offering **purchasable Extras cover** for general treatment services. These are separate products purchased in addition to the base OSHC tier.

### Extras Tiers

| Tier | Annual Limit | Key Services |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Freedom 50 | Lower limits | Basic dental, optical, physio |
| Freedom 60 | Mid-range | Dental, optical, physio, chiro |
| Budget 60 | Budget option | Selected general treatments |
| Your Choice 60 | Flexible | Choose your benefit mix |
| Top 60 | Higher limits | Comprehensive general treatment |
| Top 75 | High limits | Extended dental, optical, allied health |
| Top 90 | Highest limits | Maximum general treatment cover |

The number (50/60/75/90) generally indicates the percentage of the service cost covered, subject to annual sub-limits per service type.

### Extras Coverage Categories (typical)
- General dental (check-ups, fillings, extractions)
- Major dental (crowns, bridges, dentures)
- Optical (glasses, contact lenses, eye exams)
- Physiotherapy
- Chiropractic
- Podiatry
- Psychology
- Remedial massage
- Acupuncture
- Natural therapies

**Note**: Extras cover has its own waiting periods (typically 2–6 months for general dental, 12 months for major dental). These are separate from OSHC hospital waiting periods.

## Cost Calculation Impact

For a student with Bupa OSHC:

```
Out-of-hospital GP visit:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee  (zero if doctor charges at MBS rate)

Out-of-hospital specialist/pathology/radiology:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee  (zero if doctor charges at MBS rate)

In-hospital (MGS doctor):
  OSHC pays = Doctor_charge (no gap)

In-hospital (non-MGS doctor):
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Pharmaceuticals:
  Student pays PBS co-payment first
  OSHC reimburses up to $50 per script above co-payment
  Annual cap: $500 (single) / $1,000 (family)
```

**Key advantage**: Bupa covers 100% MBS for ALL outpatient services (not just GP). Combined with the Medical Gap Scheme for in-hospital, students may have lower out-of-pocket costs for specialist services compared to insurers paying only 85% of MBS.

## Claims Process

1. **Via Bupa app or member portal**: Submit claims digitally
2. **Direct billing**: Many doctors and hospitals bill Bupa directly
3. **Manual claim**: Submit receipts with membership details
4. **Reimbursement**: To nominated Australian bank account

## Transferring to Bupa

- Obtain clearance certificate from previous OSHC provider
- Waiting periods already served are credited (continuous cover required)
- No gap between previous and new policy permitted

## Key Definitions (Bupa-Specific)

- **Medical Gap Scheme (MGS)**: Arrangement where participating doctors agree to limit charges above MBS fee, with Bupa covering the gap
- **Pre-existing condition**: Signs/symptoms in the 6 months before OSHC policy start, determined by Bupa-appointed medical practitioner
- **Emergency Treatment**: 8 specific conditions as defined in the deed
- **Agreement hospital**: Hospitals in Bupa's contracted network
