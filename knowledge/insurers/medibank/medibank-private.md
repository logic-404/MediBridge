## Tiers Offered

Medibank offers **two OSHC tiers**:
1. **OSHC Essentials** — Base-level cover meeting deed requirements
2. **OSHC Comprehensive** — Enhanced cover with higher pharma limits, mental health extras, medical repatriation, and boarder fee benefit

## Tier: OSHC Essentials

### Out-of-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| GP services (MBS-listed) | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Specialist consultations | **85% of MBS fee** |
| Pathology | **85% of MBS fee** |
| Diagnostic imaging / radiology | **85% of MBS fee** |
| All other out-of-hospital medical services | **85% of MBS fee** |

### In-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| Medical services to admitted patient | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Public hospital (shared ward, same-day, A&E, ICU, outpatient, post-op) | 100% of rate set by State/Territory health authorities |
| Agreement private hospital | 100% of contracted charges (minimum shared ward) |
| Non-agreement private hospital | Minimum benefit per Benefit Requirement Rules |

### Prescription Medicines

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Co-payment | **$30 flat** per script (NOT PBS co-payment) |
| Maximum per item | **$70** above co-payment |
| Annual limit (Single) | **$500** per calendar year |
| Annual limit (Dual/Multi-Family) | **$1,000** per calendar year |

**Key difference from other insurers**: Medibank uses a **flat $30 co-payment** per script instead of the PBS general beneficiary co-payment (currently ~$31.60). This is slightly more generous than PBS co-payment for most scripts. The $70 per-item maximum is also higher than the $50 offered by most other insurers.

### Ambulance

100% of the charge for emergency ambulance transport when medically necessary.

### Medical Devices and Prostheses

100% of the cost as listed in the Medical Devices and Human Tissue Products Rules (Federal Government Prescribed List).

### Waiting Periods

| Condition | Waiting Period |
|-----------|---------------|
| GP services (specified MBS groups) | **Nil** |
| Emergency treatment | **Nil** |
| Ambulance | **Nil** |
| Psychiatric conditions (including pre-existing) | **Nil** |
| Pre-existing conditions (non-psychiatric, hospital) | **12 months** |
| Pregnancy and birth | **12 months** (policy < 2 years) |
| All other services | **2 months** |

**Key differentiator**: Medibank (both tiers) has **nil waiting for psychiatric conditions, including pre-existing psychiatric conditions**. The deed allows a 2-month wait — Medibank removes it entirely. Similar to Bupa's waiver but stated as a standard policy feature rather than a temporary waiver.

### Exclusions

**Deed exclusions (Schedule 3):**
1. Treatment outside Australia
2. Compensable injury/illness (workers comp, motor vehicle, etc.)
3. Treatment that is not medically necessary

**Insurer-specific exclusions:**
4. Assisted reproductive services, including IVF
5. Treatment arranged before arrival in Australia
6. Pre-existing condition treatment in first 12 months (unless emergency)
7. Dental, physiotherapy, osteopathy, chiropractic, naturopathy, optical (unless MBS-listed)
8. Non-PBS medications
9. Gap between MBS fee and doctor's actual charge
10. Medical repatriation (Essentials only — not included)
11. Non-MBS mental health services (Essentials only — no extras allowance)

### Additional Features (Essentials)

| Feature | Included? | Details |
|---------|-----------|---------|
| Medical repatriation | **No** | Comprehensive only |
| Mental health extras | **No** | Comprehensive only |
| Boarder fee | **No** | Comprehensive only |
| No excess | Yes | Deed mandated |
| Medibank app | Yes | Claims, card, providers |
| 24/7 health support | Yes | OSHC Helpline |

## Tier: OSHC Comprehensive

### Out-of-Hospital Medical Benefits

| Service | Benefit Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| GP services (MBS-listed) | **100% of MBS fee** |
| Specialist consultations | **85% of MBS fee** |
| Pathology | **85% of MBS fee** |
| Diagnostic imaging / radiology | **85% of MBS fee** |
| All other out-of-hospital medical services | **85% of MBS fee** |

Same as Essentials — no difference in medical benefit rates.

### In-Hospital Medical Benefits

Identical to Essentials tier.

### Prescription Medicines

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Co-payment | **$30 flat** per script |
| Maximum per item | **100% of cost above co-payment** (no per-item cap) |
| Annual limit (Single) | **$1,000** per calendar year |
| Annual limit (Dual/Multi-Family) | **$2,000** per calendar year |

**Significant upgrade from Essentials**: Comprehensive has no per-item cap (100% reimbursement above $30 co-payment) and double the annual limits ($1,000/$2,000 vs $500/$1,000). For students on expensive medications, this is a major benefit.

### Ambulance

100% of emergency ambulance charges — same as Essentials.

### Medical Devices and Prostheses

100% of Prescribed List — same as Essentials.

### Medical Repatriation

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Maximum | **$100,000** per policy |
| Covers | Transport of student and dependants to home country |
| Conditions | Serious illness, injury, medical condition, or death |
| Approval | Must be approved by Medibank-appointed medical practitioner |

### Mental Health Extras

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Annual benefit | **$200** per person per calendar year |
| Covers | Non-MBS mental health services (counselling, psychology sessions beyond MBS cap) |

**Unique feature**: Medibank Comprehensive is the only OSHC product providing a specific annual allowance for non-MBS mental health services. This covers sessions that go beyond the Medicare-subsidised psychology sessions.

### Boarder Fee

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Maximum | **$150** per admission |
| Covers | Parent/guardian accommodation when child is admitted to hospital |

Covers the cost of a parent or guardian staying in hospital when a dependent child is admitted.

### Waiting Periods

Same as Essentials:
| Condition | Waiting Period |
|-----------|---------------|
| GP services | **Nil** |
| Emergency treatment | **Nil** |
| Psychiatric conditions (including pre-existing) | **Nil** |
| Pre-existing conditions (non-psychiatric, hospital) | **12 months** |
| Pregnancy and birth | **12 months** (policy < 2 years) |
| All other services | **2 months** |

### Exclusions

Same as Essentials, except:
- Medical repatriation IS included (up to $100,000)
- Mental health extras IS included ($200/year)
- Boarder fee IS included ($150/admission)

### Additional Features (Comprehensive)

| Feature | Included? | Details |

## Cost Calculation Impact

### Medibank Essentials

```
Out-of-hospital GP visit:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Out-of-hospital specialist/pathology/radiology:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 85%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - (MBS_fee × 85%)

In-hospital:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Pharmaceuticals:
  Student pays $30 flat co-payment
  OSHC reimburses up to $70 per item above co-payment
  Annual cap: $500 (single) / $1,000 (family)
  Student total per script = $30 + max(0, cost_above_copay - $70)
```

### Medibank Comprehensive

```
Out-of-hospital GP visit:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Out-of-hospital specialist/pathology/radiology:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 85%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - (MBS_fee × 85%)

In-hospital:
  OSHC pays = MBS_fee × 100%
  Gap = Doctor_charge - MBS_fee

Pharmaceuticals:
  Student pays $30 flat co-payment
  OSHC reimburses 100% above co-payment (no per-item cap)
  Annual cap: $1,000 (single) / $2,000 (family)
  Student total per script = $30 (until annual cap reached)

Mental health (non-MBS):
  OSHC pays up to $200/year for non-MBS sessions
  Student pays remainder above $200 annual cap
```

### Worked Example: Expensive Medication ($120 PBS price)

**Medibank Essentials**:
- Student pays: $30 co-payment
- Remaining: $120 - $30 = $90
- OSHC reimburses: min($90, $70) = $70
- Student total: $30 + ($90 - $70) = **$50**

**Medibank Comprehensive**:
- Student pays: $30 co-payment
- Remaining: $120 - $30 = $90
- OSHC reimburses: $90 (100%, no per-item cap)
- Student total: **$30**

**Other insurers (PBS co-pay ~$31.60, $50 max)**:
- Student pays: $31.60 PBS co-payment
- Remaining: $120 - $31.60 = $88.40
- OSHC reimburses: min($88.40, $50) = $50
- Student total: $31.60 + ($88.40 - $50) = **$70**

## Claims Process

1. **Medibank app**: Submit claims digitally with photo of receipt
2. **Member portal**: Online claims submission
3. **Direct billing**: Many GPs and hospitals bill Medibank directly
4. **In-person**: Visit a Medibank retail store
5. **Post**: Mail receipts with claim form
6. **Reimbursement**: To nominated Australian bank account

---

## Transferring to Medibank

- Obtain clearance certificate from previous OSHC provider
- Waiting periods already served are credited (continuous cover required)
- No gap between previous and new policy permitted

## Key Definitions (Medibank-Specific)

- **Pre-existing condition**: Signs/symptoms existed in the 6-month period before OSHC start, determined by Medibank-appointed medical practitioner
- **Emergency Treatment**: 8 specific conditions as defined in the deed
- **Agreement hospital**: Hospitals in Medibank's contracted network
- **Boarder fee**: Cost of parent/guardian accommodation during child's hospital stay
- **Non-MBS mental health**: Psychology/counselling sessions not covered under Medicare (e.g., sessions beyond the annual MBS cap)
